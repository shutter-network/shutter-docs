Empowering Fair Play in Blockchain

Shutter is an open-source encryption protocol that has been in development since March 2021. The cutting-edge Shutter protocol is designed to enhance fairness and security in decentralized ecosystems through threshold encryption and Distributed Key Generation (DKG).

By encrypting transactions or votes until they are fixed in the blockchain, Shutter ensures that everyone has access to the same information simultaneously, preventing front running, censorship, and manipulation.

Shutter promotes base layer neutrality, information symmetry, and accessibility. It is a versatile tool for combating Maximal Extractable Value (MEV) exploitation and enabling fairer governance in DAOs. With applications from secure voting to time-lock encryption, Shutter is poised to become the go-to solution for decentralized encryption services, ensuring a more equitable and secure blockchain environment.

By preventing information asymmetry and fostering a level playing field, Shutter not only enhances security but also promotes a more transparent and equitable blockchain ecosystem, making it a superior choice for those seeking to safeguard their decentralized applications and governance processes.

A Distributed Solution​

Shutter enables fair play in blockchain by using threshold encryption and Distributed Key Generation (DKG) to safeguard transactions and votes from manipulation. In a Shutterized system, users encrypt their actions until they are securely added to the blockchain, guaranteeing that everyone can access the same information simultaneously.

One of the essential elements of Shutter's technology is its network of Keypers. These are trusted nodes that work together to create and share decryption keys only after transactions are confirmed. This decentralized approach ensures that no single entity can control or manipulate the transaction process, which strengthens the system's neutrality and security.

Shutter distinguishes itself from competitors by effectively tackling the key concerns surrounding Maximal Extractable Value (MEV) extraction without introducing centralization risks. Many purported "solutions" to MEV result in centralization by necessitating trust in a single party, potentially leading to censorship and increased exploitation. Shutter sidesteps these issues by upholding a completely decentralized encryption mechanism, thereby guaranteeing the preservation of integrity and fairness in blockchain interactions.
